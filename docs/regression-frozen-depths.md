# M6 regression checklist

- Player slides on ice and stops normally after leaving it.
- Pushable boxes slide on ice.
- Player enters water with a splash and can swim using Jump/Up and Down.
- Player has unlimited breathing.
- Boxes and barrels float rather than falling straight through water.
- Water extinguishes a lit barrel fuse.
- Underwater barrel explosions have a visibly smaller radius.
- Enemies move slowly and sink in water.
- Projectiles slow down in water.
- Ladders, jump pads, pressure plates, conveyors, one-way platforms and falling platforms still work.
- All three JSON levels load successfully.
