# M5 regression checklist

- A brief touch shorter than 0.15 seconds does not trigger the platform.
- Standing continuously for 0.15 seconds starts the countdown.
- Leaving after activation does not cancel the countdown.
- Stone uses an 8 second countdown unless JSON overrides it.
- Cracks/shaking or warning lights become visible near the end.
- Player, pushable boxes, and enemies can each trigger it.
- The platform stops being solid when it begins falling.
- It disappears below the world and respawns after the delay.
- Respawn waits while the original area is obstructed.
- Wood, stone, metal, and ice have visibly different rendering and timing.
- Ladders, moving platforms, one-way platforms, conveyors, jump pads, pressure plates, crates, barrels, and shooting still work.
