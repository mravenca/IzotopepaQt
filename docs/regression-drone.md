# Drone regression checklist

- Existing walkers, shooters, jumpers, and boss still spawn.
- Drone follows its patrol route.
- Drone detects and pursues the player within vision range.
- Drone fires the configured burst and respects reload time.
- Player bullets and explosions damage the drone.
- Drone death awards score once.
- F3 displays drone state, health, cooldown, vision, and waypoint.
- Levels 1-10 still load.
