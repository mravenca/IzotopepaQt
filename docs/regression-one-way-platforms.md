- [ ] Player lands on the platform while descending.
- [ ] Platform sides do not block horizontal movement.
- [ ] Down + Jump drops through exactly once.
- [ ] Normal jump still works when not standing on a one-way platform.
- [ ] A jump-pad-launched box passes upward through and lands on descent.
- [ ] Boxes resting on one-way platforms remain stable.
- [ ] Jumping enemies pass through from below and land from above.
- [ ] Ladders, moving platforms, conveyors, jump pads, and pressure plates still work.
