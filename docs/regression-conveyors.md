# M3 conveyor regression checklist

- Player is carried in the direction shown by the animated arrows.
- Left-moving and right-moving belts both work.
- Player can walk against a belt and can jump off normally.
- Pushable boxes move when no player input is applied.
- A box can still be pushed with or against the belt.
- Boxes do not pass through walls, doors, crates, barrels, or other boxes.
- Pressure plates detect boxes delivered by conveyors.
- Jump pads launch boxes arriving from conveyors.
- Moving platforms, ladders, crates, barrels, doors, and enemy combat still work.
- Restarting a level restores all boxes and conveyor positions.
