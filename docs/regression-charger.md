# Charger regression checklist

- Charger patrols within its configured limits.
- It only detects the player in front and within vertical tolerance.
- Warning state is visible before every charge.
- Wall impact causes a stun and recovery cycle.
- Player contact damages the player once and stuns the Charger.
- Pushable boxes stop the charge and are knocked away.
- Crates break and keep their configured drops.
- Barrels ignite on impact.
- Jump pads launch the Charger.
- Water cancels charging.
- Conveyors and ice influence movement.
- Falling platforms respond to Charger weight.
- Bullets and explosions damage it.
- Score is awarded exactly once.
- F3 shows Charger state and timers.
- Existing drones, turrets, and legacy enemies remain functional.
